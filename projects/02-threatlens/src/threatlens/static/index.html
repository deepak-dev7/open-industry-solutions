MITRE ATT&CK Mapping & Detection Principles

Enterprise Threat Framework
T1110.001

Brute Force: Password Guessing

Adversaries systematically guess passwords to gain unauthorized access to target accounts. ThreatLens correlates bursts of login failures originating from the same source targeting identical accounts.

T1110.003

Brute Force: Password Spraying

Adversaries attempt a small number of commonly used passwords against a wide enumeration of corporate accounts to bypass account lockout policies. ThreatLens correlates single-IP attacks spanning multiple distinct users.

T1078

Valid Accounts: Impossible Travel

When valid credentials are leaked or bought on the dark web, authentications may occur from widely separated geographies within impossible transit windows. ThreatLens uses Haversine great-circle velocity math to flag impossible spatial anomalies.

T1078.004

Valid Accounts: Cloud / System Accounts (After Hours)

Privileged system logins (such as root or domain administrator) occurring during off-hours or weekends often signify lateral movement or unauthorized insider access.

T1071

Application Layer Protocol: Anonymization

Threat actors frequently route attacks through Tor exit nodes and bulletproof proxy networks to disguise source attribution. ThreatLens checks incoming traffic against curated threat intelligence feeds.
